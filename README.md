# Aleph Labs

> An invention and engineering institution turning meaningful problems into real technologies.

Aleph Labs exists to discover, invent, engineer, and develop technologies with the potential to become enduring ventures and companies.

We operate at the intersection of research, engineering, invention, and venture creation.

## Our model

Aleph Labs develops meaningful technological ideas from early research and invention through engineering, validation, and venture formation.

Some technologies may remain within Aleph Labs.

Others may mature into independent ventures or companies while remaining connected to the institution that created them.

## Ventures

Aleph Labs maintains a small number of deliberate engineering and venture programs rather than an uncontrolled collection of projects.

Current flagship engineering work includes:

- **APC** — an authorization-continuity and finality enforcement technology.
- **APC Website** — the controlled public commercial experience for APC.
- **APC Sandbox** — the safe demonstration and validation environment.
- **APC Pilot** — controlled customer and integration engineering.
- **APC Deployment** — deployment and release promotion infrastructure.

These boundaries are intentional.

## Engineering principles

We build systems that are:

- security-first
- evidence-driven
- deterministic where correctness requires determinism
- independently testable
- operationally observable
- explicit about limitations and maturity
- designed for long-term evolution

Production claims require evidence.

A repository, interface, test, placeholder, or document alone does not constitute production readiness.

## Institutional principle

Aleph Labs is the institutional home for invention and engineering.

A venture may eventually become a separate company, but its technology, governance, and engineering history must remain traceable.
