What We Build

ALEPH works across technology and science, including:

- Artificial intelligence
- Computing
- Cybersecurity
- Systems engineering
- Distributed systems
- Automation
- Scientific and technical research
- Emerging technologies
- New technological infrastructure

Our repositories contain the actual work:

research → experiments → prototypes → systems → products

When a technology proves capable of becoming something larger, it may evolve into an independent product, platform, or company.



How We Work

We care about both first principles and final outcomes.

We question what exists.

We investigate why it works.

We invent what is missing.

We engineer what can be made reliable.

We build what can be tested.

We commercialize what creates real value.

We scale what proves itself.



Open Work

Where appropriate, ALEPH publishes its work openly so that it can be examined, tested, reproduced, challenged, and improved.

Repositories are organized around the work itself—not around appearances.



Why ALEPH?

א — Aleph

Aleph is the first letter of the Hebrew alphabet.

We use it as a symbol of beginning:

something that does not yet exist can become real through knowledge, invention, engineering, and persistent execution.

That is what ALEPH exists to do.
